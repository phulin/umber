# Tooling surface disposition

Status: owner decision for `umber2-vgjr.18.1`

This inventory decides which package, command, benchmark, trace, prototype,
fuzz, and associated script surfaces may be changed by `umber2-vgjr.18.2`
through `umber2-vgjr.18.4`. It is not a deletion list. In particular, absence
from routine CI is not retirement evidence: explicit live-reference,
performance, profiling, and fuzz tiers are intentionally outside
`cargo test --tests`.

## Dispositions

- **Supported compatibility** is an operator-facing command or public Rust
  surface whose retirement has not been approved. It survives until an
  equivalent command is proved or a separate compatibility decision retires
  it.
- **Active gate** has an assertion, budget, comparison, publication, or
  failure-classification contract. A gate may be explicit and opt-in rather
  than part of routine CI.
- **Reproducible diagnostic** answers a current engineering question with a
  pinned workload or protocol but does not itself decide pass/fail for the
  repository.
- **Historical surface** records a completed choice and has no surviving
  compatibility, gate, or diagnostic authority. This document approves only
  these rows for deletion by `umber2-vgjr.18.4`; that issue must still verify
  the stated dependencies and preserve the cited receipt in documentation or
  Git history.

An _external invocation_ below means the documented command available to a
maintainer outside a Cargo test. “None documented” is a negative audit result,
not permission to delete the surface.

## Script disposition summary

The candidate-specific rows govern their scripts. For clarity:

- `scripts/regen-fixtures.sh`, `scripts/check-tools.sh`,
  `scripts/check-latex-parity.sh`, `scripts/run-latex-parity.sh`,
  `scripts/check-node-width-budget.sh`, and
  `scripts/check-snapshot-budgets.sh` are **active gates** or the sole supported
  entry point to one.
- `scripts/bench-plain-tex.sh` and
  `scripts/build-tex82-document-traces.sh` are **reproducible diagnostics**.
- `scripts/test-incremental-fuzz.sh` and
  `scripts/effectful-rollback-fuzz.sh` are explicit **active gates**.
- No script is historical merely because CI does not call it. The three
  owner-approved historical surfaces were retired by `18.4`; every other row
  in this inventory remains supported compatibility, an active gate, or a
  reproducible diagnostic.

## Handoff

- `umber2-vgjr.18.2` may move the reference-process kernel into fixturegen and
  contract parity around comparison/triage, but must preserve or separately
  retire the two compatibility surfaces identified above.
- `umber2-vgjr.18.3` may retire the two completed layout migration commands and
  registries only as an explicit compatibility change. The shared cohort
  transaction and ordinary regeneration modes survive.
- `umber2-vgjr.18.4` rehomed layout and width workloads under `tex-typeset`
  and the pure-memo edit workload under `tex-incr`, without changing workload
  or baseline bytes. Its fresh audit found no intervening caller or authority,
  so it retired only the three approved historical surfaces.
